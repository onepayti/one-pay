Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed.
The arrangement of type involves selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths, line-spacing, and letter-spacing. The term typography is also applied to the style, arrangement, and appearance of the letters, numbers, and symbols created by the process.
